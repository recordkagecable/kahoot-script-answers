## 🌟 Overview

**Kahoot Script Answers** is a **study and review tool** designed to help students learn from Kahoot quizzes *after* they've been played in class. It focuses on **understanding the material**, not on cheating during live games.

The tool lets you load a completed quiz, review every question and correct answer side-by-side, take self-check quizzes, and build study notes from what you got wrong.

> ⚠️ **Academic integrity notice:** This project is intended for **legitimate studying and review only**. Using any tool to gain an unfair advantage during a **live, graded, or competitive quiz is academic dishonesty** and violates Kahoot's Terms of Service and most schools' codes of conduct. **Do not use this during live Kahoot games.** Use it to learn, not to cheat.

## 🏆 Why This Tool

<div align="center">

| 💎 **Advantage** | 📝 **Description** |
|:---|:---|
| 🎓 **Learning-First** | Built to reinforce knowledge through repetition and review |
| 📖 **Clear Explanations** | Shows not just the answer, but why it's correct |
| 🧠 **Self-Testing** | Practice mode with instant feedback and scored results |
| 🛡️ **Ethical by Design** | Features intentionally exclude live-game automation |
